# Units #

## Charge ##

The amount of charge

The unit is **Coulomb**
The definition is actually defined by the Amp.  The definition is the amount of charge transported by **one** Amp of current in **one second**.

### Relation to elementary charges ###

An *elmentary* charge is the charge of a single electron (or photon).  It is denoted by e.

$e = 1.602 * 10^{-19}\space C$ 

So 1 Coulomb of charges is $6.242 * 10^{18}$ elementary charges

## Current ##

Current is $charge/time$

The unit is the **Amp**

$
Amp = Coulombs / second 
$

## Voltage ##

Voltage is $energy / charge$.

Energy transferred per unit of charge.

I is also known as potential difference and can be thought of as a pressure or force.

$1 \space Volt = 1 \space Joule/Coulomb$

## Power ##

Power is $energy / time$

The **rate** of energy transfer.  With the unit of energy being the **J**oule.  The unit of Power is the **Watt**

$1 \space W = 1 \space J/s$

### Relation ship with voltage and current ###
$P = V * I$


So in terms of voltage and current, we get

Power (Watts) = Voltage ($J / C$) * Current ($C/s$) = $J/S$ (energy/sec)
